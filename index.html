What's inside

Everything a thinking space needs

Linked notes

Connect any two notes by writing [[Note title]]. Links to notes that don't exist yet render dashed — click one to create the note on the spot.

The Graph

Your whole knowledge web, drawn live — notes as stars, links as threads between them. Drag stars to rearrange, tap one to open that note.

Effortless writing

Just type — your note is always styled. Formatting buttons handle headings, bold, italics, and lists. No syntax to learn, no preview mode.

Tags, search & progress

File notes under tags, search everything live, and watch progress rings fill as more of your notes join the web.

AI assistant ✦

Summarize any note in one click, or ask Saturn a question and get an answer drawn only from your own notes. Free, powered by Gemini — just sign in with Google.

Dead Stars bin

Deleted notes wait in a recoverable bin as dim stars. Restore them anytime, or remove them for good.

Your data

Saturn is local-first. Your notes live in your own browser's storage — nothing is uploaded to a server, and every visitor gets their own private notebook. Signing in with Google gives you a separate notebook per account on this device (notes don't sync between devices yet). Only when you explicitly use an AI feature are the relevant notes sent to the AI for that single request.
